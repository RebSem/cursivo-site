# Cursivo

## Register

brand

## Users

Car rental owners and operations teams, especially Dubai operators managing multilingual customers and multiple branches. Source: README.md, GEMINI.md, and the existing landing copy.

## Product Purpose

One rental-specific AI agent and CRM: customer messages become structured leads, rental offers and manager tasks. The September 2026 prototype also announces a planned open MCP interface for connected agents to check inventory, compare terms and prepare bookings.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed, attentive. The user requests Apple-level presentation, retaining the product's substance. Show the product with generous space and clear typography.

## Anti-references

Avoid overloaded forms and visual clutter, as specified by DESIGN_SYSTEM.md. Avoid unsupported market-first claims and invented conversion statistics. Clearly distinguish an illustrative integration from a working backend.

## Design Principles

- Make the product understandable on the first screen.
- Demonstrate the path from a conversation to a manager-ready lead.
- Explain MCP in terms of rental outcomes.
- Keep human control and branch context visible.
- Present a local prototype for review before production adoption.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve the repository's responsive and reduced-motion conventions. Use semantic headings, visible keyboard focus, meaningful labels and keyboard-operable controls. Existing production locales are English, Russian and Arabic. The review prototype includes Russian and English; existing Arabic pages stay available.

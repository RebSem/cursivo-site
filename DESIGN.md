# Cursivo prototype design

## Direction

A rental owner reviews Cursivo on a laptop in a bright office between customer calls. Use a light, precise product presentation, with one graphite section to announce the agent ecosystem. Reference: the user's Apple brief, with Cursivo's existing warm accent and Manrope identity.

## Color

Restrained strategy. Near-white cool neutrals, graphite text, terracotta for selected brand details, green for availability. OKLCH tokens. Dark panels retain sufficient contrast for supporting text.

## Typography

Existing self-hosted Manrope for body and display. Large tightly spaced headlines, calm paragraph measure and small but legible interface labels. Native font stack fallback.

## Layout

Maximum reading canvas 1140px. Open hero, substantial interactive CRM preview, asymmetric product features, dark MCP demonstration, short onboarding sequence, FAQ and direct founder contact. Generous section gaps and deliberate variation in density.

## Components

Graphite pill primary actions, text secondary links, thin borders and subtle shadows on product windows. Rounded corners belong to application surfaces, not to every text block. Mobile navigation is a native disclosure.

## Motion

Short transform/opacity transitions. One-time progressive section reveals with visible content as the default. All interactions remain immediate and reduced motion is respected. No perpetual decorative animation.

## Prototype boundary

New review routes /prototype/ and /prototype/en/. Product demos use explicit sample data and never contact a booking backend. All founder links retain the existing site's contact destinations.
